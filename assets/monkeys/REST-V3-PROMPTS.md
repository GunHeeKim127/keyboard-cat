## capuchin

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: production repair sheet for the SAME cute desktop primate character. Reference image shows its established appearance; change ONLY the SLEEP and CARRY poses, keep identity, fur colors, head/body proportions and pixel-art style.
Subject: white-fronted capuchin, cream-white forehead and face, honey brown coat, long curled tail.
Output one precisely aligned sprite sheet, FOUR columns and TWO rows = EIGHT isolated complete poses on true transparent background. Each row must have exactly four whole animals, with generous gutters. No labels, numbers, guide marks, frames, floor, shadow, background or watermark. Same head and torso scale throughout, whole face/tail/toes fit in every cell.
TOP ROW: four frames of ONE identical sleeping pose with only tiny breathing changes. curled on its side with cheek resting clearly on both folded forearms; long tail loosely around body, face visible. Keep the head in the SAME orientation and location in all4; no turning head, flipping face, eye opening, leg shifting, body growing or melting. Anatomically clean four limbs folded compactly, no extra toes or severed body parts. The face and closed eyes must remain readable at small icon size.
BOTTOM ROW: four frames gently HANGING FROM A SHORT HORIZONTAL WOODEN BRANCH. The branch MUST be visibly drawn in EVERY frame, brown wood with dark outline, extends just beyond both hands, same position/length/thickness across all4. Both hands visibly wrap around and TOUCH the branch, curved fingers over top with thumbs opposing; not fists floating below empty air. Small natural elbow bend, torso below, knees comfortably tucked, calm cute face. No neck grabbing, no ears attached to branch, no impossible wrist angles, no other limb floating away. All four poses keep branch/hands in the same location; only gentle breathing or tiny blink. Tail naturally hanging softly below or beside body. The branch is a required part of the silhouette, not a backdrop, and must be wholly included and uncut in each cell. No extremely tall pose or giant branch that makes the monkey tiny.
Pixel-art finish, warm cute character, simple readable anatomy, consistent lighting and scale. Eight complete whole-body raster poses; never separate limb pieces.
